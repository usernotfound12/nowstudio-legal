What the app does

Pivot is a founder's showcase and a peer feed in one. You browse pitch decks as a swipeable carousel, discover other founders in the room over a direct device-to-device connection and beam your pitch to them, and — once you hold verified-business-owner status — post real problems to the Problems feed, file feedback on other startups, answer other people's problems and climb the points leaderboard.

Is there an account to create?

No. Pivot has no sign-up, no login and no server. That also means there is nothing to restore if you move to a new phone.

My posts disappeared after I closed the app

That is how this version behaves rather than a bug. Pivot keeps what you enter — applications, problems, feedback, responses and points — in memory for the session, and does not save it to disk or to iCloud. Relaunching starts from the built-in content again. Please do not use it to hold anything you need to keep.

How do I become a verified business owner?

Open the owner verification screen and submit your business name, role, contact and a short note. Verification comes back a few seconds later and unlocks posting to the Problems feed. It is worth knowing that this review runs inside the app on your device — your application is not sent to anyone, and the applications you review as a verified member are the samples that ship with the app. The privacy policy sets that out in full.

Nearby discovery is not finding anyone

Nearby needs the local network permission, which iOS asks for the first time you switch discovery on, and it needs the other person to have Pivot open with discovery on as well. Both devices should have Wi-Fi and Bluetooth enabled, since Apple's peer-to-peer networking uses them to find each other even without a shared network. If the permission was declined, re-enable it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. Some of the founders shown on the radar are built-in sample entries, so a name appearing there does not always mean a real device is nearby.

Restoring a purchase

Purchases are tied to your Apple Account. Reinstall, open the app and use Restore Purchases. If nothing appears, check you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it on.

A permission prompt was declined

Local Network can be re-enabled at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security. Until then Nearby discovery and beaming stay unavailable; the rest of the app works without it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. We hold nothing on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete.
