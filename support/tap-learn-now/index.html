Set up a learning label

  1. Open Labels and add the starter objects or create your own object.
  2. Add the words and optional sentences you want spoken. Starter vocabulary includes English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Hindi; other custom speech languages are available.
  3. Open an object and choose Write this label to a sticker. Hold the top of your compatible iPhone near a writable NDEF sticker.
  4. Place the sticker on the object. In Learn, tap Scan a sticker before each scan, then hold the iPhone near it.

Children and adult learners

Child mode plays English words. Grown-ups opens a multiplication question to return to setup; this is a simple gate, not a security lock. Adult learners choose a learning language. You can also select an object directly without a sticker, play its word slowly or hear an example sentence. No microphone is needed and pronunciation is not scored.

NFC and speech troubleshooting

NFC requires a compatible physical iPhone with iOS 18 or later. iPad supports editing and playback, not NFC scanning. This version scans while the app is open; it does not automatically speak from a locked phone or background tap. Use stickers designed for metal on a metal fridge door. Try one sticker at a time and hold the top of the phone close. A locked or incompatible sticker cannot be written.

Existing sticker data is preserved unless you choose Replace sticker data and confirm it. The app reads back the written ID before confirming success. Changing a translation does not require rewriting the sticker.

Speech uses the voices available on your device. Check volume, test Play word, and choose an installed voice. Some voices need to be downloaded through iOS speech settings. Keep small stickers away from children who could swallow them.

Backup and another phone

Export a library backup from Labels and import it on another phone to recognize the same sticker IDs. Libraries do not sync automatically. Existing local edits take priority during import. Backups contain your words and practice history; keep them private. Export before deleting the app or changing devices.
