Getting started

Open Borrow to create a loan and share its iCloud invitation. The other person can paste the invitation link to accept. Check that both devices have iCloud enabled. Borrowers report a return; owners confirm it. Stop sharing through the loan’s share controls. Important data should be exported before removing the app.

Get help

Email info@nowstudioai.com with the app name, app version, operating system and steps that caused the problem. Please remove private details from attachments.

Purchases

If a purchased feature is missing, use Restore Purchases in the app and check that the same Apple Account is signed in. Apple manages billing and refund requests.
