How the game works

Each puzzle shows a set of clue words that all connect to one hidden word. Type the word that links them. Play on one phone — solo or passing it between players — or start Race Online and go head to head with a second phone, where both screens reveal the same clues at the same moment and the faster correct answer takes the round.

Devices can't find each other

Race Online connects the two phones directly, so both need Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on and to be near each other. One phone hosts, the other joins. iOS asks for Local Network permission the first time — if you declined, re-enable it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. Only two phones can pair: once a race is joined, the app stops accepting anyone else.

The race sits on "Syncing clocks…"

Before the first round the two phones measure the delay between them so the clues appear at the same instant on both screens. It is meant to take about a second. If it stalls, leave the race and start it again with both phones close together and off any crowded Wi-Fi network.

My answer was marked wrong

Guesses are checked against the puzzle's answer on your own phone, ignoring capitals and surrounding spaces. If a word you think should count is being rejected, send us the clue words and the answer you tried and we will look at the puzzle.

Playing on a TV

Connect the phone to a TV or projector by AirPlay or a cable and the shared board moves to that screen while the phone keeps your own input. Nothing needs to be set up in the app — it switches over as soon as the display connects.

A permission prompt was declined

Local network can be re-enabled at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security. Until then the feature that needs it stays unavailable — the one-phone modes still work normally.

Deleting your data

There is nothing to delete. Word Link Now saves no scores, settings or history to your device; everything disappears when you close the app.

Reporting a problem

Tell us what happened, what you expected, and the steps that led there. Screenshots help. info@nowstudioai.com
