DialNow Support

An audio guestbook for events · Now Studio LLC

Email info@nowstudioai.com and we will get back to you. Below are the questions we are asked most.

For guests

Do I need to download anything?
No. Scan the host's code with the Camera app and DialNow opens straight at the record button. No app, no account, no sign-in.

Nothing happened when I scanned the code.
Make sure you are on an iPhone running iOS 17 or later and connected to the internet. If you tapped a link rather than scanning, open it in Safari. On Android or a computer you will see a page explaining that DialNow is an iPhone app.

Can I stay anonymous?
Yes. The name field is optional; leave it blank and your message arrives as "Anonymous guest".

Can I re-record?
Yes, as many times as you like before you tap Send. You can also leave more than one message.

Who can hear my message?
Only the host and any co-hosts they invited. Your recording is encrypted on your phone before it is sent, and we cannot open it.

For hosts

Why do I need to be signed into iCloud?
Your guestbook is stored in your own iCloud account rather than on our servers. That is what keeps your guests' voices yours. Guests do not need iCloud — only you do.

My guests recorded messages but I don't see them.
Open the event and tap Collect messages, or pull down to refresh. Messages are collected on demand rather than pushed.

How do I share the guestbook with my partner?
Open the event, tap Invite a co-host, and send them the link. They will see every message and can manage them too.

Can I stop collecting messages?
Yes — turn off Collecting messages in the event. Guests who scan the code then see a friendly "closed" screen.

How do I save a message?
Tap the ••• button next to any message and choose Save or share audio.

What happens if I stop using the app?
The guestbook lives in your iCloud account, so it stays as long as that account does. Apple provides no way to transfer ownership of shared data, so co-hosts lose access if your account goes away. Save anything irreplaceable to your device.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Hosts need an iCloud account; guests do not.
