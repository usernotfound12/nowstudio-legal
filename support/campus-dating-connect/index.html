What the app does

Campus Dating Connect is an invite-and-vetting dating app. You sign in with Game Center, record or type a short intro, and apply to the community; existing verified members review applications. Once you are in, you can browse profiles, join live matchmaking sessions with people who are online right now, and chat with your matches. Chats and shared photos sync between the two matched people through their own iCloud accounts.

Why do I need Game Center?

Game Center sign-in is the app's check that there is one real Apple Account behind each profile, which is what keeps bot and throwaway accounts out. The app reads only your Game Center display name and player identifier — not your email, your real name or your game history. If the gate will not clear, open Settings › Game Center, sign in there, then return to the app and tap the retry option on that screen.

I applied and I am stuck waiting for approval

Applications need approvals from three different verified members before they go through, and two flags will reject one. Until then your application sits in the queue and the app shows it as pending. Use Check Again on the waiting screen to refresh your status. An invite code from an existing member counts as one of your three approvals, so a code makes the wait shorter. This step needs iCloud, so make sure you are signed in to iCloud in Settings.

Who can see my application?

Other verified members can. That is the point of the review queue — they see your display name, age and bio in order to approve or flag you. Please read the privacy policy before you write your bio, because that section is deliberately not private.

My match cannot see my messages

Chat sync runs on Apple's CloudKit sharing, so both of you need to be signed in to iCloud and online. The other person also has to have opened the share invite for the conversation — if they never did, open the chat, tap the options button and use Sync to iCloud (Share Link) to send it again. New messages from your match arrive while the conversation is open, so leave the chat on screen for a few seconds if you are waiting on a reply.

My voice intro will not record or transcribe

The intro needs both microphone and speech recognition permission, and it needs between fifteen and thirty seconds of speech. Both can be re-enabled in Settings › Privacy & Security. If either stays off, or if speech recognition is unavailable on your device, you can skip the recording and type your bio instead. The recording itself is deleted as soon as it has been turned into text.

Restoring a purchase

Purchases are tied to your Apple Account. Reinstall, open the app and use Restore Purchases. If nothing appears, check you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it on.

A permission prompt was declined

Microphone and Speech Recognition can be re-enabled at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security. Until then the feature that needs them stays unavailable, and you can type your bio rather than record it.

Reporting someone, or asking us to remove your data

Email info@nowstudioai.com and tell us who and what happened. The same address handles data removal: we can delete the community-vetting records the app stored for you in the shared CloudKit database — your applicant record, and the approvals, flags and invite codes tied to it. We cannot reach your conversations, because those live in your own iCloud account and not on our servers.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your profile, matches and messages from that device. Content already synced into a shared conversation stays in the two participants' iCloud accounts until removed there, and your vetting records need the email above.

Reporting a problem

Tell us what happened, what you expected, and the steps that led there. Screenshots help. info@nowstudioai.com
