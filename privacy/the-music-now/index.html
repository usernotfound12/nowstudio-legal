The Music Now Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026 · Now Studio LLC

The short version. This app asks for microphone — and only while you are using the feature that needs it. None of it is sent to Now Studio.

What we collect

  • Microphone — Music Now listens to audio only while a tuner or analyzer is open.
  • Audio and PDF files you choose — Tone Match can analyze an audio file you pick, and the score library can import sheet music PDFs. The app receives only the files you select.

Tone Match

Tone Match compares two short clips — a reference and your own — and suggests an EQ move. Each clip is either recorded through the microphone (up to twelve seconds) or imported from an audio file you pick (the first thirty seconds are read).

What happens to those clips is worth being precise about. The audio is held as raw samples in the app's memory while it is being analyzed and so you can play the clip back. It is never written to a file, never saved to your library, and never uploaded anywhere. An imported file is read in place and is not copied — the original stays wherever you keep it, untouched. Recording again, resetting, or closing the app discards the audio, and the analysis itself is arithmetic performed entirely on your device against the clips you supplied: there is no lookup service and no network request involved.

Where it is stored

  • On your device — your practice settings and your tuner and metronome preferences. Tone Match clips are not stored at all, as described above.
  • iCloud — sheet music you import into the score library is kept in this app's iCloud Drive folder when iCloud is available, and practice settings and streaks sync through your own iCloud account. It lives under your Apple Account, not on our servers, and Now Studio has no access to it. With iCloud unavailable, the score library falls back to local storage on the device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We are told that a purchase succeeded so the app can unlock; we never see your payment details. Purchase state is managed with RevenueCat, a third-party purchase infrastructure SDK, configured without an account identifier so it works from an anonymous id it generates itself.

What we never collect

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that profile you. We do not collect device identifiers, browsing history or usage data, and we never sell or share data. This is verifiable rather than a promise: the app contains no analytics or advertising frameworks.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
