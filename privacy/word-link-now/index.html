Word Link Now Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026 · Now Studio LLC

The short version. This app asks for local network — and only while you are using the feature that needs it. None of it is sent to Now Studio.

What we collect

  • Local network — Word Link Now uses your local network and Bluetooth to find and connect to a nearby player for Race Online mode.

Where it is stored

  • Nowhere, once you close the app — scores, streaks and the puzzles you have seen are held in memory for the length of a game and nothing more. The app writes no saved games, settings or history to disk. There is no account, no iCloud sync and no Game Center leaderboard.
  • Between the two phones in Race Online — a race connects the two devices directly to each other over Apple's peer-to-peer networking. Race data never passes through a server of ours, and neither phone keeps a copy of it after the race ends.

What Race Online actually sends

Only what a race needs, and only to the one phone you are paired with: the name iOS reports for your device when the two phones find each other, a short exchange of clock timestamps so both screens reveal the clues at the same instant, a “ready” signal, the round's clue words and answer from the app's own bundled puzzle set, and — once your round resolves — whether you got it right and how many seconds it took.

The words you type are not sent. Your guess is checked against the answer on your own phone; all that crosses to the other device is right-or-wrong and a time. There is no chat, no photos, no contacts and no sign-in anywhere in the app.

AirPlay and external displays

Connecting a TV or projector moves the shared puzzle board onto that screen while your phone keeps your private input. That is drawing to a display you already connected — nothing is sent anywhere, and the app does not choose or reach the display itself.

What we never collect

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that profile you. We do not collect device identifiers, browsing history or usage data, and we never sell or share data. This is verifiable rather than a promise: the app contains no analytics or advertising frameworks.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
