Cart Pause Now Privacy Policy

Now Studio LLC · Updated October 27, 2026

Your content

Return links, receipts, deadlines and refund records stay in the app’s local storage. Local notifications and Live Activities show information you choose to track. Opening a retailer link sends you to that retailer, whose privacy policy applies.

In-app purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an anonymous app identifier for feature access and purchase analytics. We do not receive your payment-card details.

RevenueCat privacy policy

Control and deletion

You can delete the content you no longer need. Removing local data does not delete copies previously exported, shared or backed up elsewhere. Apple manages iCloud and device backups. Contact us for help with data you have sent directly to support.

Support

If you email us, we receive your message and any attachments so we can answer it. Send only what is needed; remove personal details from screenshots.

Advertising and tracking

These features do not sell your notes or use them for cross-app advertising. We do not request an advertising identifier for them.

Contact and changes

Privacy questions can be sent to info@nowstudioai.com. We update the date on this page when the policy changes.
