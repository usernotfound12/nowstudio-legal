Trace Letters Now Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026 · Now Studio LLC

The short version. This app asks for local network — and only while you are using the feature that needs it. None of it is sent to Now Studio.

What we collect

  • Local network — Trace Letters Now uses your local network and Bluetooth to connect the two phones in Play Together mode.

Where it is stored

  • On your device — the two player names you type in Settings and your chosen round length are saved on the phone so they survive a restart. Scores live in memory for the length of a game. That is the whole of what Trace Letters Now stores, and none of it leaves the device except as described below. There is no account, no iCloud sync and no Game Center leaderboard.
  • Between the two phones in Play Together — the two devices connect directly to each other over Apple's peer-to-peer networking. Game data never passes through a server of ours, and nothing from a session is written to disk on either phone.

What Play Together actually sends

Only what the game needs, and only to the one phone you are paired with: the name iOS reports for your device when the two phones find each other, then a running snapshot of the round — round number, whose turn it is, the two player names and scores, the round length, and the last round's result — plus the guesser's tapped letter going back, and requests to reveal or move to the next round.

The secret letter is only ever sent to the phone that is tracing it. The snapshot the guessing phone receives has no field for the letter at all, so the guesser's device never even holds a message that could carry it. There is no chat, no photos, no contacts and no sign-in anywhere in the app.

AirPlay and external displays

When an Apple TV or other display is connected, it shows only the current turn, the timer and the score — the secret letter never appears there. That is drawing to a display you already connected; nothing is sent anywhere, and the app does not choose or reach the display itself.

What we never collect

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that profile you. We do not collect device identifiers, browsing history or usage data, and we never sell or share data. This is verifiable rather than a promise: the app contains no analytics or advertising frameworks.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
