Pivot Business Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026 · Now Studio LLC

The short version. Pivot has no account, no server and no cloud storage. Everything you type stays in the app on your device. The one time anything leaves your phone is when you switch on Nearby discovery, and then it goes directly to another phone in the room over the local network — never through us.

There is no account and no backend

Pivot does not ask you to sign up, sign in, or give an email address. It has no server of its own, and it does not use iCloud or CloudKit. Nothing you write in the app is transmitted to Now Studio, because there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to.

Where your entries are stored

In the app's own memory while it is running. Pivot writes nothing to a database, nothing to iCloud and nothing to a file of its own. Anything you add during a session — a verification application, a problem you post, feedback you file, a response you write, the points you earn — exists for that session and is gone once the app is closed and relaunched. That is a real limitation to know about as much as it is a privacy property, so please do not treat Pivot as a place to keep something you need to keep.

The pitch decks and founder profiles are sample content

The founder profile, pitch carousel, metrics and VibeCheck card the app shows as yours are built-in demonstration content that ships inside the app. They are not assembled from anything you entered and not drawn from your device. The same is true of the other founders you browse, the nearby founders shown on the radar, and the sample applications sitting in the review queue.

Verified business owner applications

Applying for verified-business-owner status asks you for a business name, your role, a contact detail and a short note. Here is what actually happens to that, stated precisely, because it is easy to assume otherwise: the application is added to the queue held inside the app on your device, and it is not sent anywhere. No other person receives it. The peer review that follows — the approvals that arrive a few seconds later and flip you to verified — is produced by the app itself on your device to demonstrate the flow. There is no network call and no other real reviewer involved.

The corollary is worth saying too. When you review applications as a verified member, the applications you are approving or rejecting are the sample entries that shipped with the app, not submissions from real people.

The Problems feed

Problems, feedback, responses and the points leaderboard all work the same way: they live in the app on your device for as long as it is running. Posting to the feed does not publish anything to anyone.

Nearby discovery and beaming a pitch

This is the only feature that sends anything off your device, and only while you have deliberately turned discovery on. It uses Apple's peer-to-peer networking over the local network, directly between phones, with no server in the middle. The permission prompt you will see says, in the app's own words:

  • Local network — “PIVOT uses your local network to discover nearby founders, beam pitch carousels, and surface VibeCheck social cards at events — no Wi-Fi required.”

While discovery is on, the app broadcasts your founder name, company and card colour so other nearby devices running Pivot can list you. If you then beam your pitch to someone, your founder profile and its pitch slides are sent straight to that one device. The connection between the two devices requires encryption. Nothing that passes over it is stored by us or routed through us, and turning discovery off stops the broadcast and clears the list.

What the app does not use at all

No camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no photo library, no health or calendar data, no notifications. The app requests none of those permissions because nothing in it needs them. It uses haptics, which needs no permission.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We are told that a purchase succeeded so the app can unlock; we never see your payment details. Purchase state is managed with RevenueCat, a third-party purchase infrastructure SDK. It is set up without an account identifier, so it works from an anonymous id it generates itself — the app does not pass it your name, your business details or anything you write in the app.

What we never collect

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no SDK that profiles you or builds an ad audience from you. We do not collect device identifiers, browsing history or usage data, and we never sell or share data. This was verified against the source rather than assumed: the only third-party component in the app is the RevenueCat purchase SDK described above, and there is no analytics or advertising framework anywhere in it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting your data

Closing the app clears what you entered during that session. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. There is nothing held on our side to delete.
