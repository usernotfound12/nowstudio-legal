DialNow Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026 · Now Studio LLC

DialNow is an audio guestbook. A host creates an event; guests scan a code and leave a voice message. This policy explains exactly where those recordings go.

The short version. Voice messages are stored in the host's own iCloud account, not on our servers. Every recording is encrypted on the guest's phone before it is sent, using a key only the host holds. Now Studio cannot listen to any message — not as a policy, but because we do not have the key.

What we collect

  • Voice recordings made by guests, and any name or note a guest chooses to add.
  • Event details a host enters: event title, host name, date, prompt, and an optional cover photo.

That is the complete list. DialNow contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, or usage data.

Where it is stored

  • Messages and events live in the host's private iCloud database, inside their personal Apple Account. Now Studio has no access to it.
  • Event invite details (title, host name, prompt, cover photo) are stored in a public Apple CloudKit database so a guest's phone can display the invite before they record. They are readable only by someone who has the event's invite code.
  • A message in transit passes briefly through a relay we operate at nowstudioai.com. Apple does not permit anonymous writes to CloudKit, so this relay performs the write on the guest's behalf. It receives the recording already encrypted, forwards it, and retains nothing.

Encryption

Each event has its own key pair. The public half travels with the invite; the private half never leaves the host's iCloud database. A guest's recording and name are sealed on their phone with AES-GCM under a key derived from that pair (X25519 key agreement, HKDF-SHA256). Only the host's device can open them.

Guests

Guests need no account, no app install, and no sign-in. Providing a name is optional — a guest may stay anonymous. Nothing about a guest is collected beyond the recording and whatever they chose to type.

Sharing and deletion

  • A host may invite co-hosts, who can see and manage the same messages.
  • A host may delete any message, or the whole guestbook, at any time. Deleting a guestbook removes it for co-hosts too.
  • Because data lives in the host's iCloud account, deleting the app does not delete the data; deleting the guestbook inside the app does.
  • We cannot delete a host's data on request, because we cannot reach it. Apple's iCloud privacy terms govern that storage.

Children

DialNow is rated 4+ and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information beyond what is described above.
