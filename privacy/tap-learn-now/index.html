Tap Learn Now Privacy Policy

Now Studio LLC · Updated October 27, 2026

Your learning library

Tap Learn Now stores object names, rooms, illustrations, words, example sentences and optional practice records locally in its app storage. We do not receive that library. The app does not create accounts, include an analytics or advertising SDK, track you across apps, or capture microphone recordings. Words are played using Apple’s device speech voices.

NFC stickers

A learning sticker stores a random object identifier, not your translations, name or practice history. Someone with a copy of your library can recognize its identifiers. The app does not locate or continuously monitor stickers. Scanning and writing occur when you start an NFC session.

Children

Parents or guardians set up stickers and choose the words children hear. Child mode presents English learning controls and a simple grown-up gate for setup. We do not collect children’s profiles, voices or learning records. This gate is not authentication or a substitute for adult supervision.

Exports and backups

If you export a library, its JSON file includes your words and practice history. Sharing or saving that file sends it to the recipient or storage service you choose. We do not operate a library-sharing server or cloud sync. Apple may include app data in device backups according to your device settings.

Control and deletion

Delete an object to remove its words and related practice records from this device. Deleting the app can remove its local library. Deletion does not erase copies you previously exported, shared or saved in device backups. NFC stickers remain physically rewritable unless they were locked elsewhere; the app does not permanently lock them.

Support

If you choose to email support, we receive your email, message and attachments to respond. Send only what is needed and remove children’s names or other private information from screenshots. Contact info@nowstudioai.com for privacy or support questions.

Changes

We update the date on this page when the policy changes.
