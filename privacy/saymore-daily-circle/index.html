SayMore: Daily Circle Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026 · Now Studio LLC

The short version. This app asks for microphone, location and speech recognition — and only while you are using the feature that needs it. None of it is sent to Now Studio.

What we collect

  • Microphone — So you can speak your answer instead of typing it. SayMore never stores or sends the recording — only the words it turns into, which you can edit before sharing.
  • Location — SayMore checks your approximate location once to place you in the right city queue — Discover opens one city at a time. Your street-level location is never requested, and location is never checked in the background.
  • Speech recognition — To turn what you say into text. SayMore uses on-device recognition only, so your voice never leaves your phone.

Where it is stored

  • iCloud — Your content syncs through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. It lives under your Apple Account, not on our servers, and Now Studio has no access to it.
  • Sign in with Apple — Apple gives the app an identifier for you and, if you allow it, your name. We never receive your Apple Account password.

What we never collect

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that profile you. We do not collect device identifiers, browsing history or usage data, and we never sell or share data. This is verifiable rather than a promise: the app contains no analytics or advertising frameworks.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
